# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Stardew Valley players running the Stardew Valley Expanded mod, working toward the fish collection, Master Angler and the Perfection Tracker. They keep the tracker open beside the game (second monitor, laptop, or phone), usually in evening play sessions, and glance at it between in-game days.

## Product Purpose
A single-file, offline Perfection tracker for Stardew Valley 1.6 + SVE. It reads the player's save file in the browser and shows every Perfection category (shipping, fish, cooking, crafting, friends, monsters, stardrops, walnuts, farmer level, obelisks, Gold Clock) plus bundles and the museum. The core questions it answers at a glance: "How close am I to Perfection, and what should I do today?" Manual ticking works without a save. Success means the player never opens the wiki mid-session.

## Brand Personality
Cozy, precise, quick. Feels like a well-made companion app for the game: warm lamplight on a night dock, not a spreadsheet and not a pixel-art costume.

## Anti-references
- The source Google Sheet: dense rows, checkboxes, multiline cells.
- Wood-panel / parchment "Stardew skin" fan sites that imitate the game UI and hurt legibility.
- Generic SaaS dashboards with hero metrics and card grids.

## Design Principles
- Answer "what can I catch right now" before anything else.
- One tap to log a catch; never lose progress (local persistence, export, undo).
- Spoilers are opt-in: SVE story locations stay hidden until the player chooses.
- Dense but scannable: visual encodings (season dots, time bars, difficulty) over text.
- Works offline and on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, full keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced-motion support, color never the only signal (season and weather also carry labels/icons).
